## Separate current-output comparison

`focus-history-current-output.ts` loads the immutable reviewed index and every
complete output/receipt by its pinned hash, authenticates original wires and
compares both actual fresh legacy streams to their current legacy baseline and
both actual fresh native streams to their complete refusal baseline. It does
not compare legacy and native for equivalence. Native refusals cannot confer
whole-product provenance or native handling.

The existing full-T1 source-built capture runs once, using the real shared
build helper and genuine original configured APIs. Its raw capture remains
`unreviewed` under the unchanged exploratory schema. A separate
`current-output-report.json` embeds that complete actual capture, per-attempt
whole-byte differences and current-only authority/accounting. It is written
before aggregate success assertions, preserving failed attempts and output
drift. Neither snapshots nor expected bytes are automatically updated.
Compilation/receipt failure keeps the original build logs and failure packet;
no successful observation is fabricated.

Pure laws cover immutable input/receipt/index/output custody, positive full
metadata/message/geometry/help/label/fix/requery drift, complete negative
outputs, full native context drift, failed first-attempt preservation, false
summary/provenance/authority, and missing/extra observations. The original
execution stays excluded from T0 and included in every full T1; this change
adds neither a second product build/capture nor a performance pipeline stage.
Exact-head Actions, full protected current comparisons and literal merge are
still required for this source change; local pure checks grant no runtime
acceptance for its future candidate.
