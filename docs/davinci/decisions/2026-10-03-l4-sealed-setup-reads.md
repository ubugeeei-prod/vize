## Decision

external render declaration. Existing DOM grouping, eligibility, binding order,
block demand and change decisions still come from L3. Existing assembly owns
the complete import preamble and its transform-compatible order.

The private accessor consumes the exact ordered original occurrence pointers
and File binding owners. The target validates its actual node, lexical scope,
retained AST, decoded source, authored range and coordinate identity before
the existing transactional expression writer can append a value. There is no
public caller accessor, runtime-mode flag, `_ctx` fallback, upper-level normal
dependency, initializer/ref/purity heuristic or second AST walk.

This first source-spelling slice admits retained literal roots and direct
Identifier roots whose one genuine resolver occurrence has the exact semantic
identifier spelling and retained root span. The O(1) shape check preserves
checked surrounding block comments and trivia. Compound roots and escaped
reference spellings return `UncertifiedExpressionSpelling`; L4 does not trim,
parse or inspect hidden member/key descendants to broaden admission. A real
same-complete-walk resolver spelling fact is the next prerequisite.

`emit_file` retains its independent literal-only contract. Declaration
membership and `emit_vue` alone do not certify original template construction
or a complete SFC product. The real frontend must retain and admit its original
opaque native SFC ownership before exposing any product route.
