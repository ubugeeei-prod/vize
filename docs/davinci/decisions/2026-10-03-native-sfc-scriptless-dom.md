# Explicit native scriptless SFC DOM product

as an explicit native entry. The product adapter stays in `crates/`; its
normal dependencies point to the real levels in `davinci/`. The L4 backend
retains no container policy or parser. Paired comments belong to #6880 and
#6838 in the same delivered change.

complete module or a typed stage refusal. It obtains admission only through
that original descriptor/Program/Component/File owner. The sole same-file
`NativeFileAnalysis` enters `emit_file`; its complete render declaration then
enters the existing neutral `module::assemble` with `RenderPlacement::Function`.
For the scriptless family, that assembler supplies its established empty
