it, then its original list consumes that same comma. The Doc walk emits that
comma once. A preceding expression requires a checked separator before its
next element; a preceding Elision already owns that separator. Hole identity
and order distinguish `[,]` from `[]` and `[a,,]` from `[a,]`.
