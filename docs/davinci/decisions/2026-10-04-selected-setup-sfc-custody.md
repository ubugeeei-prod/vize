scripts, every style, custom/external blocks, duplicate or unsupported

construction. The existing scriptless entry and its all-script/style refusals

node is minted. No eligibility scanner, AST replay, second binder or second

entry; callers cannot supply it or claim completion with a flag.

and TemplateView to that same completed selected File and owning setup receipt.

while the view or its moved template completion lives. Refusals preserve the
