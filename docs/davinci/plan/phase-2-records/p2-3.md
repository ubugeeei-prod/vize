# P2-3 — `PassObserver`

**The seven-hook observer and the four in-tree observers. Landed 2026-08-19;
every acceptance clause met, one contract step landed earlier than its
dependency expected.**

## The law this task exists for

**A fused pass's cost is never attributed to a walk it did not make.**

The failure is quiet rather than loud, which is why it needs a pinned law
rather than care. Three fusable passes fused into one traversal made _one_
walk. An observer that opened a measurement per pass would report three; the
numbers would look entirely plausible; and every decision downstream — which
pass to optimize, whether fusion paid for itself, whether
`budgets.toml [traversal]` is being held — would be made against a cost that
was never spent.

So every `PassEvent` carries the `FusionGroup` its pass landed in, plus
`is_group_entry` / `is_group_exit` marking the passes that open and close the
walk, and `group_members` naming every pass sharing it. An observer that wants
per-walk accounting keys on `group_index` and opens at the entry; one that
wants per-pass accounting keys on `pass_index`. Both are available and neither
is guessed.

The law is pinned twice: `tests/pass_observer_law.rs` asserts observed walks
equal the plan's group count over six pipeline shapes (empty, single, all
fused, barrier in the middle, two barriers, mixed) and pins the full hook
sequence including walk indices and boundary flags; `tests/pass_observer_timing.rs`
asserts it again at the level where it would actually mislead — two walks
produce two export entries, not three.

## Zero cost, and why it is stronger than the contract asked

The contract asks that attachment be checked once per pipeline, never per node.
What landed has **no attachment check at all**: `run_pipeline` is generic over
the observer, so `NoObserver`'s empty bodies inline away and the un-observed
pipeline _is_ the pipeline.

No `dyn PassObserver` is offered, on purpose. A trait object would put a vtable
call on the per-pass path and make the zero-cost claim untestable; a caller
that genuinely needs runtime choice composes an enum observer of its own.
Composition is `Pair<A, B>`, a type rather than a `Vec<Box<dyn …>>`, so static
dispatch survives it.

Pinned by the bench pair, both over the same four-pass fixture pipeline
(two fused, a barrier, one more — three walks, so the driver's loop is
measured rather than a single call):

| bench                          | allocs | wall p50 |
| ------------------------------ | -----: | -------: |
| `davinci_pipeline_unobserved`  |      0 |     6 ns |
| `davinci_pipeline_no_observer` |      0 |     6 ns |

**Alloc-identical**, which is the gate: `allocs` is exact and
machine-independent, both entries are registered in `budgets.toml [bench]`, and
`bench-compare` reports them `alloc-gated … ok`. Wall p50 is under criterion's
resolution for a routine this small on a laptop, which is exactly why the claim
is gated on the alloc side and the wall side stays report-only until the
Blacksmith recording (P0-4).

## The four observers

- **`TimingObserver`** — one attributed span per walk through
  `vize_carton::profiler`, reusing `SpanAttribution`'s `{stage, pass, …}`
  builders rather than introducing a second attribution model, which is the
  failure the P0-11 scaffolding was landed early to prevent. `stage` is the
  pipeline's; `pass` is the group's **lead** pass, because that is the walk's
  identity — which passes fused behind it is recoverable from the pipeline,
  while the fact that they shared a walk is not recoverable once thrown away.
  A failed walk's timer is **discarded**: its duration measures how long it
  took to fail, which is not the cost of the walk, and recording it would put
  a differently-meaning number under the same key.
- **`BudgetObserver`** — walks, passes, analyses, pipelines, failures. This is
  what replaces P2-12a's hard-wired probe once passes run through the manager,
  and it counts the same quantity **by construction** rather than by matching
  definitions across two implementations. `walks` increments at group entry,
  never per pass.
- **`CountingRemarkSink`** — the channel, not the content. P3-13 owns what a
  remark says; landing the sink now means a pass written before then has
  somewhere to emit, and the decision about _what_ to say is not also a
  decision about _how_. It counts applied/missed without storing text, so it
  is allocation-free and stays the release default (guardrail 5: verification
  and explanation never ship on the hot path).
- **`FolioObserver`** — a stage dump after every pass, headed by the pass that
  produced it. The header is what makes a sequence of pages bisectable:
  without it a diff says the output changed, not who changed it.

## Deviation: the folio observer did not wait for P2-4

The contract says the folio-printing observer "consumes P2-4". It does not:
the `Folio` trait has existed since P0-10, so the observer binds to it and
works today with `CroquisFolio`. What P2-4 adds is `#[derive(Folio)]` — so
every S2 type gets its page for free — and `davinci-opt --pipeline`, which is
this observer wired to a CLI. Neither changes the observer's shape, so
deferring it would have bought nothing and left P2-4 carrying a design
decision that belongs here.

## Why the schema is not re-validated here

`TimingObserver` records through `vize_carton::profiler::record_attributed` —
the same serializer path `crates/vize_carton/tests/davinci_profile_export.rs`
already validates against
[`profile-export.schema.json`](./profile-export.schema.json), with a strict
validator that **errors on any schema keyword it does not implement** rather
than skipping it. Copying that validator into `vize_davinci` would be a second
implementation of exactly the thing P0-11 exists to keep single.

So the test here proves the observer's samples reach the export with the right
key, the right count and the right attribution, and schema conformance stays
pinned where the validator lives. `vize_carton` cannot depend on
`vize_davinci` (the dependency runs the other way), so moving the assertion
there is not an option either.
