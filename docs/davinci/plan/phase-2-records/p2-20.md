## Differential-lane retirement

The environment fallbacks are gone. The remaining `davinci-differential` lanes
are verification-only lanes: enabling them dual-runs and panics on divergence;
disabling them removes the comparator, not the shipped S2 DOM path.

Phase 1's "for one release" life has been honored by the stable releases
through `v0.421.0`. Phase 2 deliberately re-dates the P2-9, P2-11 and P2-16
verification lanes into phase 3: the first phase-3 task that replaces each
corresponding old consumer path must either delete its differential lane with
zero-divergence corpus evidence or write a fresh owner/date into that task's
record. The lanes cannot expire silently at the phase boundary.
