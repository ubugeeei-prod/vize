## Summary

Two open hosts can place one workspace alias leaf at different owned targets. On primevue-volt the Button.vue member points `packages/primevue/node_modules/@primevue/icons` at `src/button/node_modules/@primevue/icons`, and FilesCard.vue points it at `src/node_modules/@primevue/icons`. The editor union then refuses to materialize:

```
corsa backend unreachable for .../apps/volt/components/landing/FilesCard.vue (Communication error: Failed to materialize Canon project union: IO error: Conflicting workspace package alias targets in editor union); respawning and retrying
corsa bridge retired after a backend failure; a fresh session will be spawned on demand
```

The fresh session then prepares that host alone, because its union is empty. In Real Project Matrix run 44107635850 this happened twice in the primevue-volt LSP lifecycle, and locally twice per run.

## Change

On that conflict only, the preparation retires the other union members and their compiled contexts, then materializes this host alone. That is the state a fresh session reaches, without the tsgo restart. Retired hosts rebuild against the current mirror on their next request; their cached contexts are evicted, so none is reused after its files left the mirror. The conflict check itself, the materializer's refusal (`editor_union_rejects_conflicting_internal_alias_identity_without_touching_target` is unchanged) and every other union failure are unchanged. The error is named once in the materializer (`VirtualProject::is_editor_union_alias_conflict`) instead of being matched by message elsewhere.

`perf`: published diagnostics are those of a fresh-session retry, as before. The only difference is that a backend restart is avoided.
