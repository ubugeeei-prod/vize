**Release-measure flake root cause (#13647, in the queue):**
- Cause: mimalloc embeds `__DATE__`/`__TIME__`. When the build time ends in 0 or 1, the linker merges the adjacent "0"/"1" strings into it, which shifts `.rodata` by 2 B and flips ~29 instruction rows. That happened in 22% of builds.
- Fix: pin `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` to the base commit time, and require byte-identical twin executables.
- Follow-up: the same pin for `level-instruction-counts.yml`, with a ceiling recheck (the agent is on it).
