# P2-5b — `ExprRef` contract, including the retained-`None` classes

**The expression reference and the decision the task exists for. Landed
2026-08-20; the retained-`None` classes resolve to candidate (a) — an
escape variant with pessimal documented semantics from day one — with
the measurement below, and the wider retained contract (candidate (b))
deliberately deferred to the task that owns its measurement. The
escape-variant semantics section is the review point; it is written for
the maintainer's judgement, not around it.**

## The decision: (a), with (b) left open on purpose

`ExprRef<'a>` (`crates/vize_disegno/src/expr.rs`) has three variants:
`Js(&'a JsExpr<'a>)` (the retained oxc AST beside its exact text and
authored span), `Foreign(&'a ForeignExpr<'a>)` (type-only until phase 6,
charter #28), and `Opaque(&'a OpaqueExpr<'a>)` — the escape variant,
carrying a classified [`OpaqueReason`], the exact text, and the span.

Why (a) and not (b) or (c) now:

1. **The variant is forced no matter what.** Two of the five measured
   classes can never carry an AST: text the nesting guard refuses
   (`vize_carton::expression_guard::expression_is_safe_to_parse` — oxc's
   recursive parser cannot be depth-limited, so the text is never handed
   to it) and text oxc rejects. Candidate (b) alone cannot make a
   two-variant `ExprRef` total; every widening of the retained contract
   only moves inputs from `Opaque` to `Js`. So the real question was
   never (a)-versus-(b) — it was whether to _also_ widen now.
2. **Widening now would pin a contract to a prediction.** The measured
   escape-bound class (the `unretained` counter) is 9.1–9.7% of corpus
   expression rewrites in both runs below, and P2-9's contract owns the
   measurement of whether region-structured lowering shrinks exactly
   that class ("a number from the existing `retained::differential`
   counters, not a prediction"). Widening
   `crates/vize_armature/src/parser/expression.rs` before that number
   exists would choose the wider contract's shape from a guess — the
   move the assurance doctrine forbids — and would move the P1-5/P1-7
   pinned counter laws in three sibling crates inside a task whose
   review point is semantics, not parser scope.
3. **Pessimal semantics make the deferral safe.** Because every consumer
   must treat `Opaque` as worst-case (below), a later widening is
   **monotone**: inputs migrating from `Opaque` to `Js` can only give
   consumers _more_ information, never contradict what they assumed. A
   consumer written against the pessimal laws today is still correct
   after any (b). This is the property LLVM's `undef` lacked, and it is
   what makes (a)-now-(b)-later a sequence rather than a compromise.

## The escape variant's semantics (the review point)

Written in `crates/vize_disegno/src/expr/opaque.rs` as five numbered
laws, present from the first commit — the imported LLVM `undef`/`poison`
rule is that an escape value with underspecified semantics accretes
contradictory assumptions, so the semantics here are **pessimal on every
capability query**:

1. **May read anything** — assumed to reference every binding reachable
   in its scope; no pass may prove a binding unused, unshadowed, or dead
   across one.
2. **May do anything** — assumed arbitrarily effectful; no dropping,
   duplicating, reordering, or speculating.
3. **Never constant** — no folding, hoisting, or caching
   (`OpaqueExpr::is_constant` is a `const fn false`).
4. **Equal to nothing, itself included** — textual equality never
   implies semantic equality, so no CSE, dedup, or memoization keys on
   an opaque expression. Encoded in the type system: neither
   `OpaqueExpr` nor `ExprRef` (nor any op payload containing one)
   implements `PartialEq`; identity-needing consumers compare the owned
   folio mirror, which is explicit about carrying text, not semantics.
5. **Emission is byte-verbatim or refusal** — the only sound emission is
   the source text unchanged into a position whose grammar accepts it
   verbatim; a target that cannot must fail with a diagnostic, never
   guess.

The stated consequence: every transformation of an opaque expression is
unsound _by definition_; valid handlings are pass-through and
diagnostic. A pass that wants to do better must first move the input out
of the class via (b), which law 4's monotonicity makes always-safe.

The reasons are a closed enum with a documented classification split:
`NestingRefused` and `ParseRejected` are **text-classifiable** (the only
refusals the load-path parse site produces), while `ForValue`,
`MultiStatement` and `Compound` are **position-classified** — grammatical
facts about where the text came from, assignable only by the lowering
that saw the position (P2-8), never recoverable from the text. `ForValue`
carries the `a in b in c` disagreement in its rustdoc (JS `in`
associates left; Vue's v-for grammar splits at the first viable
`in`/`of`), which is the P1-6-recorded reason a retained AST of a v-for
value must never be consumed naively.

Honest labels on this run:

- The absolute totals are **not comparable** to the committed P1-9 run:
  the fixture tree's hydration state differs (12 of the corpus

  the **shares**, which moved by at most one point. Both runs say the

  dominated by `unretained`.
- The counters bucket coarser than the contract's five classes. The
  mapping to `ExprRef` is: `unretained` (9.1% of all calls in the P1-9
  run, 9.7% here) is the `Opaque`-bound aggregate of all five classes —
  the split among them is **not machine-measured today** and P2-8's
  position-classifying lowering is where it becomes measurable.
  `dialect_rejected` and `ts_strip_rewrote` carry retained ASTs (the
  refusal is the legacy splicer's TS-node gate, a consumer limitation)
  and are therefore **`Js` under `ExprRef`**, not escape classes.
  `params` is a params-position validation (slot props), not an
  expression position at all; S2 has no such position until the
  `ui.bind`-class ops land (P2-9), and the classes apply there when it
  does.

## The `Js` payload: one recorded deviation

The architecture names the variant `Js(&'a oxc_ast::ast::Expression<'a>)`.
Landed as `Js(&'a JsExpr<'a>)` where `JsExpr { ast, source, span }` —
the AST reference is exactly the architecture's, one field away —
because the **same task's** folio clause requires slice + span at print
time, and an AST pointer alone can recover neither (a standalone-parsed
expression's oxc spans are text-relative, not file-relative). This is
the shape P1-5 already learned (`vize_relief::JsExpression` carries
`raw` beside `ast`), restated without a relief dependency.

## The folio payload and the replay law

Expression positions serialize as owned text + span, never an AST
(P1-11's contract; the debug arena-generation stamp in
`crates/vize_carton/src/allocator/generation.rs` is the mechanism that
makes in-place survival impossible): `js("…" @s:e)`,
`opaque(<reason> "…" @s:e)`, `foreign(<dialect> "…" @s:e)`, grammar in
[`folio-format.md`](../folio-format.md). A `js` payload re-parses into
the arena on load through `JsExpr::parse_in` — the second and last home
of the P1-5 admission rule (guard → parse → whole-coverage), shared
byte-for-byte with armature's template-parse site. Two decisions there:

- **Load is total.** `ExprRef::parse_js_in` never fails: text the parse
  site refuses loads as `Opaque` with the text-classified reason —
  which is what a hand-edited or stale folio has earned, and the MLIR
  one-shot rule (total functions, no rollback) applied to loading.
- **No `davinci.expr.parses` increment.** The P1-5 counter law
  (`counter == distinct expressions`) is a statement about the compile
  path; a folio load is tooling re-entry. Counting it would break the
  law without protecting anything it protects.

The replay law is `crates/vize_disegno/tests/expr_replay.rs`: build in
a pooled arena → mirror → print → **drop the `pool::acquire()` guard**
(arena reset) → parse → structural equality — plus the reload of the
retained payload into a second pooled arena, printing the identical
page. `ForeignExpr::facts` (the dialect side tables) deliberately do
not serialize: the folio payload for `Foreign` is dialect + source +
span per this task's contract, and the phase-6 dialect contract owns
the facts' vocabulary _and_ spelling — part of what type-only means.

## The capability contract

`crates/vize_disegno/src/expr/capability.rs`: `ExprDialect` with the
four duties (enumerate referenced bindings + an exactness witness,
classify const-ness, map spans, emit for a target), resolved **once per
file as a generic parameter** — the P2-3 static-dispatch shape; no
`dyn ExprDialect` is offered (guardrail 1; the architecture's one
designated `dyn` seam is S4 emitters). Per-variant answering is
documented on the trait: the core answers for `Js`, the pessimal laws
fix the answers for `Opaque` (an implementation must not best-effort
scan opaque text), and no implementation ships until phase 6.

**The P1-8 note, encoded as required at the time:** `enumerate_bindings`'s
rustdoc named it as where the single identifier-extraction implementation
would live (issue [#4365](https://github.com/ubugeeei-prod/vize/issues/4365),
`scanner-parity-report.md`) and stated that the trait encoded neither waiver
resolution. The later P1-8 follow-up resolved that blocker without changing the
phase-2 record's guardrail: no second ad-hoc scanner belongs here.

## Sizes, pinned

All 64-bit, guard rationale unchanged. New: `ExprRef` 16 (tag + shared
payload pointer), `JsExpr` 32, `OpaqueExpr` 32, `OpaqueReason` 1,
`ForeignExpr` 64, `ForeignFact` 32. Moved by the 16-byte reference
(from the zero-sized `ExprSlot`): `InterpolationOp` 8 → 24, `IfBranch`
40 → 48, `ForOp` 40 → 96, `ForBinding` 2 → 64, `DynamicName` 16 → 24,
`SlotOp` 48 → 56, `BindingContract` 0 → 32, `ModelOp` 32 → 64,
`VueDirectiveOp` 80 → 88. Unmoved: `Op` and `BindingOp` 16 (payloads
stay boxed), `Region` 24, `TextOp` 24, `ElementOp` 104, `ComponentOp`
96, `Attribute` 40. The ratchet worked again: three first-guess figures
(`ForeignExpr`, `ModelOp`, `VueDirectiveOp`) were corrected by the
assert failing, not by measurement after the fact. Drop-freedom is
restated as `!needs_drop` const asserts on `ExprRef` and every new
payload type.

## The canary, proved by injection

Per the P0-7 staleness rule, run twice on this tree — once per new enum:

1. A `CanaryProof(&'a JsExpr<'a>)` variant injected into `ExprRef`
   broke the **crate's own** matches first: E0004 at `expr.rs`
   (`mnemonic`, `source`, `span`) and `folio/owned/expr.rs` (`own_expr`),
   4 errors. With those patched, the build failed again **only** in
   `tests/op_family.rs` (`expr_keyword`, E0004).
2. A `CanaryReason` variant injected into `OpaqueReason` broke
   `opaque.rs` (`mnemonic`; `from_mnemonic`'s `_ => None` is the
   deliberate lenient direction — unknown folio text is a parse error,
   not a match) and, patched, only `tests/op_family.rs`
   (`reason_keyword`).

Both injections were then removed; the committed tree carries no trace
(`grep -rn Canary crates/vize_disegno` → 0).

## Left open, with owners

- The position-classified reasons (`ForValue`, `MultiStatement`,
  `Compound`) are assignable only by the S1→S2 lowering — P2-8, whose
  contract already consumes this decision for v-for.
- The per-class split inside the `unretained` aggregate, and whether
  region-structured lowering shrinks it: P2-9's measurement. The (b)
  widening decision follows that number; `steps/expression/reparse.rs`
  is not deleted here (non-goal, restated).
- `ForeignExpr.facts`' folio spelling and the first `ExprDialect`
  implementation: phase 6 (charter #28).
- Slot-props/`ui.bind`-class expression positions (where the `params`
  counter class will land): P2-9.
- Observed while landing: [`phase-2-records.md`](../phase-2-records.md)'s
  index table was missing the P2-4 and P2-5a rows their tasks owed it;
  this PR adds only its own row per the minimal-plan-diff rule, and the
  gap is flagged to the owners rather than silently absorbed.
