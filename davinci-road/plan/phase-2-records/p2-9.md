## The series, closed (2026-08-21)

Seven installments, 2026-08-21 start to finish, one arc:

- **The absorbed-shares progression.** The series began expecting to
  port transforms and mostly measured them already gone: v-if
  three-quarters absorbed by the P2-8 lowering (1), v-for absorbed
  whole (2), v-slot split three ways around a codegen-heavy old tree
  (3), transform_text and five of the element family's six step files
  **dead in the shipped lane** with the living behaviour at parse and
  codegen time (4, 5), hoist_static live and split three ways (6), and
  the legacy pair live, feature-gated, and split by the dialect-op test
  (7). The recurring result: the pass bodies are small because the
  lowering is where the meaning already lives; what a pass owns is the
  law-holding consumption (hygiene, compound tiling, filter splits) and
  the facts beside the tree.
- **The classification census** — the review point, answered pass by
  pass: **five `MandatoryLowering` barriers** (v-if the canonicalizer;
  v-for, text, and legacy the preserving kind — the recorded taxonomy
  tension, four occurrences, never smoothed into a fourth kind; v-slot
  the diagnosing kind), **one `MandatoryDiagnostic`** (v-model, the
  fault table as the removal's preserving twin), **one
  `Optional`/`Fusable`** (hoist-static, the first non-barrier group),
  and the legacy barrier riding only the per-dialect
  `TRANSFORM_LEGACY`. Fusion never bought a walk (every neighbour of
  the one fusable pass is a barrier) — measured, const-pinned, and left
  as the open door P2-2 designed.
- **The facts inventory** (what realization compiles from):
  `IfFacts`, `ForFacts`, `SlotFacts` (+ the `Synthesized` origin
  producer), `TextFacts` (+ the `Compound` opaque producer),
  `ModelFacts`, `StaticFacts`, `LegacyFacts` (+ the `LegacyFilter`
  opaque producer) — each with provenance, each validated by its pass
  against the lowering's records on every run.
- **The differential lane** grew from 18 templates and an if-projection
  to a 90-template battery plus a 19-template legacy battery across
  seven projections (chains, fors, slots, text units, binding surfaces,
  hoist decisions, filter structure), with the corpus lane at **12,017
  of 12,021 templates compared, zero divergence, on every installment**
  (4 named hard-parse-error skips; every skip class counted, never
  silent) and every corpus run repeated twice with byte-identical
  counters. Two real lowering bugs (split-adjacency, installment 4) and
  two real design corrections (`StaticFacts::foreign`, the
  authored-blank value) came out of the corpus canaries, not review.
- **The residual class**: measured twice per installment, **12.73%
  unmoved seven times** — structural, recorded once (no S2 output feeds
  a shipped `rewrite_expression` site) and cited since. The prepared
  feed above is the series' answer; the number moves when P2-5b's
  widening or P2-11's read consumes it.

**The gaps ledger, with owners** (each first recorded in its
installment, consolidated here): comments in S2 (entities, in-table
construction, and comment output — the three measured S1-scope faces;
P2-11's dev-mode rendering is the forcing point), the classifier seam
(`classifier_templates` 222 — a pre-P2-11 decision), the weaker const
rule (`consts_templates` 96 — a shared allowlist home if P2-11 wants
the hoists), wrapper facts (the `<template v-for>` key and the 111
conditional slot carriers — P2-11's keyed-fragment/dynamic-slot
realization), pattern-params scope names (#4365's seam,
`models_pattern_scope` 214), `v-once`/`v-memo`/`v-html`-family ops
(land with the stage that reads them — P2-11), the `inline` root arm
(comparable when a script-integrated comparator exists), and the legacy
narrowings (filter positions, compound re-admission, outlet sugar,
0.x/1.x lines — installment 7's ledger).

**The exit-gate hand-off**: the shipped lane never changed — the S2
lane ran beside it for the whole series, `VIZE_DAVINCI_TRANSFORM`
(named once, `vize_ricalco::pass`) disarms the dual-run and is deleted
with the old lane at the phase-2 exit gate (P2-20's deletion list, the
old transform directory included); the three recorded mirrors (the
v-for splitter, the filter splitter, the legacy capability view) are
deleted with it. The realization inventory — everything the seven
records marked "P2-11" — is the DOM-backend installment's opening
worksheet.
