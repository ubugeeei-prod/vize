# P4-5c — check surface has no migrated field reads

**Index stays open.** The acceptance sentence asks the consumption
matrix for zero direct field reads of every migrated group. P4-3a's
amendment (2026-09-22) keeps two classes out of that migration, and
both still show resolved `Croquis.bindings` sites. They are not
P4-5c consumers.
